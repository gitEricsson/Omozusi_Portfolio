About

I'm a writer and a medical student. Since 2021 I've written SEO articles on crypto and Web3, ghostwritten press pieces and whitepapers for new projects, scripted YouTube videos on science, true crime and celebrity news, and co-authored peer-reviewed research.

Studying medicine shapes how I work: go to the primary source, check the detail, then make it clear for the person reading. Everything I hand over is researched and original, whether it carries my name or yours.

  • Based in Ekpoma, Edo State, Nigeria
